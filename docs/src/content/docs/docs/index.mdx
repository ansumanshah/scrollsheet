install, or jump straight to a topic below.

## Detents and control

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Detents">
    DetentSpec accepts full, medium, content, a fraction, or pixels, and onTravel reports progress
    against each one. [Read more](/docs/detents-and-control/detents)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Imperative control">
    actionsRef exposes open, close, and snapTo for deep links, push notifications, and any caller
    without a prop to drill through. [Read more](/docs/detents-and-control/imperative-control)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nested and morph">
    A Sheet.Root rendered inside another sheet's content registers as a child automatically, and
    the sheet morphs height when its content changes.
    [Read more](/docs/detents-and-control/nested-and-morph)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Scrolling and keyboard

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Scrollable content inside a sheet">
    Mark an inner scroller with data-scrollsheet-nested-scroll so a swipe scrolls the list first

  <Card title="Keyboard">

## Presentation

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Side panels">
    side anchors a sheet to bottom, top, left, or right; left and right sheets scroll on the x
    axis and their detents are widths. [Read more](/docs/presentation/side-panels)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Non-modal">
    `modal={false}` keeps the page fully interactive behind the sheet, with no backdrop, no
    focus trap, and no inert-ing the rest of the page. [Read more](/docs/presentation/non-modal)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Desktop drag">
    Mouse pointers can grab the panel anywhere and fling it; touch and pen keep riding native
    scroll-snap instead. [Read more](/docs/presentation/desktop-drag)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Desktop presentation">
    Above 768px, bottom sheets dock right as a fixed-width drawer and side sheets square off
    their lead edge; below it, sheets stay full-bleed.
    [Read more](/docs/presentation/desktop-presentation)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Page transitions">
    Mark your page wrapper data-scrollsheet-background and backgroundEffect scales, insets, and
    rounds it in sync with the sheet's travel, iOS-style.
    [Read more](/docs/presentation/page-transitions)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Migrating

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Migrate from vaul">

    Drop-in replacement for toast, useToasts, and Toaster, running on the same per-toast DOM as

  </Card>
