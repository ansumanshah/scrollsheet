---
title: Pinned footer
description: Keep a checkout button on screen at every detent with Sheet.Footer.
---

`Sheet.Footer` stays on the sheet's visible bottom edge at every detent, through a drag, and on top
of the on-screen keyboard. Your content scrolls behind it.

```tsx
<Sheet.Root detents={[0.4, 'full']}>
  <Sheet.Trigger>Checkout</Sheet.Trigger>
  <Sheet.Content className="sheet">
    <Sheet.Handle />
    <CartItems />
    <Sheet.Footer className="checkout-footer">
      <button>Pay $48.00</button>
    </Sheet.Footer>
  </Sheet.Content>
</Sheet.Root>
```

```css
.checkout-footer {
  padding: 12px 16px;
  box-shadow: 0 -1px 0 rgb(0 0 0 / 0.08);
}
```

## Where to put it

Render it as a direct child of `Sheet.Content`. The footer can't leave its parent box, so inside a
shorter wrapper it stops at the wrapper's end. Dev builds warn once when that happens.

A form is the common exception. Give it `display: contents` so it has no box of its own, and the
submit button stays inside the form:

```tsx
<Sheet.Content className="sheet">
  <form style={{ display: 'contents' }} onSubmit={pay}>
    <CardFields />
    <Sheet.Footer>
      <button type="submit">Pay $48.00</button>
    </Sheet.Footer>
  </form>
</Sheet.Content>
```

## The home indicator

The footer takes over the panel's bottom safe-area clearance, so its background runs under the home
indicator on a phone. The inset is a transparent bottom border rather than padding, which leaves
your own `padding` alone. Override the amount with `--scrollsheet-safe-area`, as on any panel.

The background defaults to the panel's color. Set your own on the footer's class.

## What it changes

- A `content` detent counts the footer's height.
- At the tallest detent and under a keyboard, the panel scrolls and the last row stops above the
  footer. A focused field scrolls clear of it too.
- With `fill`, your inner scroller ends above the footer.
- On a floating card (`--scrollsheet-inset-bottom`, or the docked desktop column) it rides the card's
  bottom edge at full height.
- Side panels and centered dialogs keep it at the bottom of the panel. A top sheet keeps it at the
  sheet's leading edge.

`--scrollsheet-footer-height` holds its measured height on the dialog, for offsets of your own.

`Drawer.Footer` is the same component, for sheets migrated from vaul.
